Talk to Our Team
​Team Identity & Service Position

Are you a trading middleman who earns profits from product price differences?
No. We are a buyer-exclusive international procurement execution team, representing only the buyer's interests throughout the process. We do not earn product price differences, accept factory rebates, or have any hidden fees. We only charge clearly displayed service fees, and all factory quotations are fully transparent and verifiable.


What can your team do?
We are a team composed of local Chinese and foreign members, based in China. We can use Chinese, English, Russian, and German as working languages.
We do not follow the trading company model — we do not buy low and sell high to make a margin. Instead, we provide execution services for you in China: helping you visit factories, assisting with online and offline negotiations. When you cannot travel frequently or conduct on-site research, we handle these tasks for you, saving you time and costs.


What categories of procurement services are you good at?
We focus on four core industrial tracks, with years of experience and a pre-screened database of compliant factories: auto & auto parts, agricultural machinery, machine tools and generator sets. We do not undertake unrelated categories to ensure service professionalism.


How long do you provide services? What are your service models?
We offer two service models:
By project / by procurement cycle – From needs confirmation to factory coordination, negotiation, and execution, we complete the entire process from start to finish.
Monthly subscription – Suitable for clients who need long-term, high-frequency follow-up, with a fixed fee and flexible service scope.
More systematic and reliable than short-term, ad-hoc individual services.


What are your advantages compared with individual procurement agents?
Compared with individual agents, our advantages are more prominent:
① Team collaboration avoids service interruption caused by limited personal energy;
② Pre-screened factory database for higher efficiency;
③ Improved risk control system to better avoid procurement risks;
④ More professional bilingual services for smoother connection.


Do you only serve overseas buyers? Can domestic buyers cooperate?
We mainly serve overseas buyers, solving procurement pain points for four major categories in China. Domestic buyers with sourcing and audit needs for the four categories are also welcome with the same standards and pricing. 

Procurement Consultation & Cooperation Q&A

What does free consultation cover? Are there any hidden charges?
No hidden fees or mandatory purchase obligations are included in free consultation.
It generally covers demand discussion, basic verification of one potential factory, preliminary procurement guidance, indicative cost estimation and key risk analysis, helping you clarify procurement orientation before formal decisions.


Will factory contact details be provided?
Contact availability depends on your chosen service package.
For independent procurement support, we offer screened supplier contacts, quotations and relevant documents for your direct negotiation.
For full-process procurement trusteeship, we handle supplier communication on your behalf to secure efficiency, stable pricing and smooth execution.


Can I contact suppliers directly during the project?
Yes, direct contact can be arranged based on project progress and practical needs.
Unified communication minimizes misunderstanding, boosts efficiency and maintains consistent quality control.
We can assist in arranging direct connection and negotiation at proper stages if needed.


How are service fees determined?
Fees are set flexibly based on project specifics, including order volume, product category and complexity, customization demands, supply chain difficulty and service level required.
Standard bulk orders come with relatively lower service charges.
Small or complex projects require more manpower and cost accordingly.
All charges will be confirmed clearly prior to cooperation.


Can I select partial services only?
Yes. You may pick individual services such as supplier verification, factory inspection or procurement assistance.
Each demand will be assessed separately to match suitable service scope.


How will previous services be handled if further cooperation proceeds?
We will arrange follow-up cooperation in a practical and fair manner.
Previous work and expenses will be reasonably counted into the overall project based on time, workload and participation degree.
All terms will be fully communicated before proceeding. 

Factory On-site Inspection & Audit

What work does on-site inspection cover?
We conduct field research at cooperating factories, carry out business communication, qualification verification, image recording and risk inspection, and compile formal inspection reports.


Can the inspection duration be adjusted as required?

How to update project progress during inspection?

How to arrange extra factory visits and long-term inspection?
Additional visits need prior negotiation. Long-term on-site follow-up service is available. Service cycle and terms will be settled separately according to actual demands.
