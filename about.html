​ABOUT US

​We are an independent sourcing team based in China, dedicated to supporting overseas buyers in industrial procurement. We work as your long-term sourcing partner and on-the-ground team—helping you identify reliable suppliers, manage procurement execution, and maintain control over quality, cost, and delivery. Rather than acting as a trading company, we represent your interests throughout the sourcing process, providing transparent, practical support for direct factory sourcing in China.

YOUR
LOCAL PARTNER
IN CHINA 

WHY WE EXIST

China offers tremendous sourcing opportunities, but also significant complexity. With thousands of factories and intermediaries, overseas buyers often face challenges in identifying reliable suppliers, verifying manufacturing capabilities, and maintaining control over pricing, quality, and delivery. At the same time, frequent travel to China is not always practical, making supplier verification, production monitoring, and on-site coordination difficult. 

HOW WE FILL THE GAP

We act as your on-the-ground sourcing support team in China, bridging the gap between remote decision-making and local execution. Rather than trading products, we work alongside you throughout the sourcing process—helping you identify manufacturers, verify capabilities, manage production, and coordinate delivery while maintaining transparency and control.

HOW WE ARE DIFFERENT

Unlike traditional trading companies, we do not buy and resell products, nor do we take commissions from suppliers. We are not tied to any specific manufacturer or industrial group.

Our role is to act as an independent sourcing partner, representing the buyer’s interests and providing objective support throughout supplier selection, verification, production, and delivery. 

WHO WE WORK WITH

We work with importers, distributors, equipment and industrial parts buyers, cross-border e-commerce sellers, and procurement teams that require reliable local support in China. Whether you are exploring new suppliers or managing existing sourcing projects, we help bridge the gap between overseas purchasing decisions and local execution.

WHAT YOU GAIN

By working with a local sourcing team, buyers gain direct access to qualified manufacturers, greater transparency throughout the sourcing process, and more competitive pricing through supplier comparison. At the same time, local oversight helps reduce sourcing risks, improve communication efficiency, and maintain better control over quality, production, and delivery without the need for a permanent presence in China.

YOUR LOCAL AFTER-SALES TEAM IN CHINA

Product delivery is only the beginning of our partnership. From quality feedback and wear part replacements to factory communication and issue follow-up, we leverage our local supply chain expertise, language capabilities, and on-the-ground presence to coordinate efficiently with Chinese manufacturers. As your dedicated after-sales team in China, we ensure every issue is managed promptly and professionally.

OUR GOAL

OUR GOAL IS SIMPLE: TO HELP INTERNATIONAL BUYERS SOURCE FROM CHINA WITH GREATER CONFIDENCE, LOWER RISKS, STRONGER SUPPLIER RELATIONSHIPS, AND LONG-TERM SUPPLY STABILITY.
